# The Walking Dead: Onslaught

Set in AMC’s The Walking Dead universe, this game is a good old-fashioned zombie shooter focused on action and combat in VR.

![preview](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1082680/ss_774a295c53d2f9a5e6ff2194d6aa9a718667dc25.1920x1080.jpg?t=1729182817)

My focus was making the gunplay and melee combat feel right to the players through proper feedback, balancing, and intended gameplay effects. As my first project out of college, working on the game's 3Cs and equipment and taking these features from pre-production to shipping was a great learning experience. It provided me with a solid foundation for game development in Unreal for my future projects.
